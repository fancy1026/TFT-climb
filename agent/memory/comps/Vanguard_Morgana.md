FIRST RULES for Vanguard Morgana - where anything below says otherwise, these hold:
- Item plan: the DPS is Morgana - Adaptive Helm, Morellonomicon, Void Staff (MetaTFT's build); the main tank is the front tank that holds the tank items - in MetaTFT's data Sentinel, with Adaptive Helm and Protector's Vow (or Gargoyle Stoneplate, Warmog's Armor). Three items on each, by priority, not a fixed order: Morgana's before the tank's when the components make the next item of either, then they take turns; an item the components on hand make only for the tank is built at once; what is beyond those six goes on the next carry (Cassiopeia). Damage items never on the tanks.
- This is a level-8 comp (its carry costs 4): no reroll below level 8 - none at level 7, free rerolls included (they are kept: they do not expire); the executor does not run them. The one exception is the last life (a loss ends the game: all the gold is rolled). Hold the gold for level 8 and roll there. The one exception below level 8 besides the last life: from stage 4, at level 7, one reroll when the shop shows no Wisp and three or more comp units are two copies short of their next star (a Wisp, and a hit is a big upgrade) - the executor makes it.
- Titan's Resolve is a fighter's item (a Brambleback kind of carry), not Morgana's: while the comp is being chosen, a Titan's Resolve already built means Riftbeast Brambleback, not Vanguard Morgana (Coven can still be played as the pivot, for its rewards). Playing Vanguard Morgana, Titan's Resolve is not built; one already built is the item a Reforger is used on.
