FIRST RULES for Riftbeast Brambleback - where anything below says otherwise, these hold:
- Riftbeast counts only at 3, 5, 7 and 10: field the Riftbeasts you own up to the step they reach (five by level 5 when five are owned - the executor fields them). A 4th, 6th, 8th or 9th Riftbeast adds nothing: every slot no step needs goes to a champion that completes a trait tier with the units fielded - in your board reason, name the tier each non-Riftbeast unit completes. A unit whose traits all stay inactive is not fielded while one that completes a tier is owned or in the shop (MetaTFT's level-8 units of owner-eighth-slot excepted).
- No level 7 while fewer than seven Riftbeasts are owned and the XP would cost interest (take the gold below 50): the seventh slot adds little (the executor does not buy that XP). With the gold staying at 50 or more, or XP that goes on to level 8, it is not held - nor when you take path 2 of owner-hp-paths (HP 50 or less without Riftbeast 7).
- Items: this is an AD comp - about 90% of its damage is physical (Brambleback, Elder Dragon). Build no AP items and no AP penetration: never Ionic Spark, never Archangel's Staff. No AP items on a 2-star Pebbles; an AP item we hold anyway (a carousel or loot Rabadon's Deathcap) goes on a 3-star Pebbles, never on Krug.

- With Riftbeast it is easy and useful to 3-star the cheap units: buy the extra copies of Krug, Cinderling and Pebbles when the shop shows them.
- In stage 2, interest comes before spare copies of a unit that leaves the final board: selling one to reach the next 10 gold is preferred (your call).
- Gnar and Taric are both better than an extra Rakan: field them over Rakan.
- Augments: Invested++ is terrible for this comp. Use the augment reroll when no card suits the comp.
- Augments are picked for the level-8 board, not MetaTFT's final board: most of our games end at level 8 and Elder Dragon or Taric come late if at all (the augment question gives the counts). A card whose value needs a fielded 5-cost - a bonus for 5-costs, a bonus that starts only with one fielded - is worth little for this comp; judge it by what it does for the level-8 board.
- Positioning: Last Whisper's holder stands on the same side of the board as Brambleback, so the armor reduction lands on the enemies Brambleback fights.
- An item whose hits reduce the target's armor or magic resist is a support item: it goes on another attacker standing beside Brambleback, so both hit the same enemies - never on Brambleback.
